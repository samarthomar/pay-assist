# Pay Assist demo: presenter script (about 15 minutes)

Open `pay-assist-demo.html` in Chrome or Edge. It works offline. Use a 1366×768 screen or larger, full screen (F11).

**Before you start:** press **Shift + R** (or the Reset button) to start fresh.

**Moving around:** click the 6 numbered circles at the top, or press **1** to **6**. Press **B** (or the red **Rule book** button) to look up any rule code; clicking a code like BR-T02 on any screen opens it too. **?** lists all shortcuts.

---

## Opening (30 seconds)
"Today, overdue customers get a dialler call and a fixed menu of options. Pay Assist replaces that with a team of AI agents that assess every account, talk to customers about a plan built around their payday, and hand sensitive cases to people. One rule runs through all of it: **the AI talks and decides; deterministic code calculates the amounts and enforces AGL's rules.**"

## Scene 1: Morning run (2.5 min)
- **Say:** "It's 7:30. Last night's file has 12,457 accounts. Watch the agents work."
- **Click:** Use today's file → Run morning assessment.
- **Point at:** the agents lighting up in the strip; the **Guardian vetoing 206 draft extensions** (bill over 90 days).
- **Click:** Sarah Mitchell in the table → show the rules, the plan and the Guardian ticks. Optional: Download output.
- **Proves:** every account is assessed before 8 am, and every decision has a reason and a rule.

## Scene 2: Sarah's journey (4 min)
- **Say:** "Sarah owes $847.50. Her hours were cut and she's paid fortnightly."
- **Click (Engage):** Send at 9:00 am → tap the SMS → tap the link → fill date of birth → tap the code banner → Verify → Open Pay Assist.
- **Point at:** no balance is shown until she's verified.
- **Click (Resolve):** I'm having trouble paying → hours were cut → Keep going → "Option 3 is a bit too high" → Sarah's sentence → Sounds good → Visa 4821 → fill feedback.
- **Point at:** "Behind the scenes" on the right. Every amount comes from the calculator and the Guardian checks every message.
- **Proves:** $115.67 every second Thursday, in about 3 minutes, with no human.

## Scene 3: "I've already paid" (1 min)
- **Set up:** on Resolve, press **Restart journey**, then **Skip to chat**.
- **Click:** I've already paid → PayPal → receipt number.
- **Proves:** we don't chase people who have paid. Reminders pause until the payment posts, within 21 days.

## Scene 4: Life support (1 min)
- **Set up:** go to **4 Protect** and click **Margaret Hughes** in the list.
- **Point at:** **Never messaged**: 0 SMS, 0 email. A specialist calls with a Copilot call flow.
- **Click:** choose an outcome → Save to CRM.
- **Proves:** vulnerable customers go to people, never to automation.

## Scene 5: $2,640 overdue (1.5 min)
- **Set up:** on **4 Protect**, click **Priya Raman**.
- **Point at:** the 👤 check. Over $2,000, the AI may draft the plan but only a person can approve it.
- **Click:** Copilot → "Use this plan" on the lower option → Approve edited plan.
- **Proves:** AI drafts, people decide, in seconds.

## Scene 6: Distress mid-chat (2 min)
- **Set up:** go to **3 Resolve**, choose **Tom Becker** in the presenter controls dropdown, press **Skip to chat**, tap **Yes, show me my options**.
- **Click:** Customer discloses distress (presenter controls).
- **Say:** "The Guardian stops collections instantly. No debt talk."
- **Click:** Open the specialist's view → Join chat → send the first Copilot reply → Apply "Pause collections" and "Protect the account".
- **Proves:** safety first, with a warm handover and nothing for the customer to repeat.

## Scene 7: Two weeks later, then Results (3 min)
- **Set up:** go to **5 Sustain** (press **Restart** there if it has already been run).
- **Click:** Fast-forward × 3 → Sarah replies **1** on the phone → Fast-forward.
- **Say:** "Her payment failed on payday. The Sustain agent found it in 5 minutes and moved the date within the rules. The plan survives."
- **Click:** See the results → scorecard **91** → the "From this demo session" box. Optional: open the **Rule book** and show **BR-S01**, the rescue rule we propose for AGL to confirm.
- **Proves:** plans rescued before they break, and measurable value.

## Close (30 seconds)
"Every number you saw was calculated, every decision traces back to an AGL rule (open the Agent console with **A**, then Customer trace), and every sensitive case went to a person."

### If something goes wrong
- **Anything odd:** press **Shift + R**, then click the screen you need in the top row and use its **Set up** step above.
- **Chat stuck:** press **Restart journey** in the presenter controls.
- **Running short of time:** skip Scenes 3 and 4. Scene 5 still shows the human approval.

### Questions you may get
- **"Is the AI real?"** In this demo the agents are simulated, so the demo is safe and repeatable. The rules, calculations and Guardian checks are real code. The design is built for Claude as the language model.
- **"Where do the KPIs come from?"** Targets and values are illustrative pilot values from the demo script, to calibrate with AGL. Volumes, debt ageing and escalation reasons are calculated from the synthetic file.
- **"What's BR-S01?"** A proposed rule for failed plan payments (move up to 7 days). It's an open point for AGL to confirm.
